Feature: scheduled CSV exports for the reporting module

Context from the PM thread:
- Customers on the Business plan keep asking support to email them their monthly usage report.
  Support handled 212 of these requests in October, each takes ~10 minutes.
- One customer (Lumen Logistics) churned in September and named "no automated reports" in the
  exit survey.
- Idea: let account admins schedule a CSV export (daily/weekly/monthly) that gets emailed to a
  list of addresses.
- Engineering concerns: large accounts have 2M+ rows/month; emailing that as an attachment
  won't work. Probably email a signed download link valid for 7 days instead.
- Not doing: PDF exports, custom column selection, exports for the Starter plan.
- Two groups will read the spec: the support leads (want to know what changes for their team
  and when) and the backend engineers who will build it (want the data volume and delivery
  constraints).
